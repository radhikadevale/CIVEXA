How CIVEXA works

Five clear steps from the first report to the final feedback.

01

Report

A citizen reports the problem with a description, location and photo.

02

Verify

The report is reviewed and verified by the concerned authority.

03

Support

Other citizens can support the same issue to increase its community impact.

04

Prioritize & Resolve

CIVEXA calculates the priority and the right department takes action.

05

Track & Feedback

Citizens follow the progress and rate the resolution afterwards.
