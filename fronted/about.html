Why CIVEXA

Built around the problems communities actually face.

Civic problems often get reported multiple times, lose visibility, or remain difficult to track. CIVEXA brings these scattered reports into one organized system.

📍
01

Local-first reporting

Citizens can report problems in their neighborhoods with clear location, description and supporting information.

🤝
02

Community supported

People affected by the same problem can support a report instead of creating multiple scattered complaints.

📊
03

Priority driven

Issues can be prioritized using factors such as community support, urgency and people affected.
